3. Core Operational Regulations

3.1 The Anti-Splitting Directive

Large projects, single procurement orders, or related service invoices may not be split into multiple smaller purchase orders or partial payments to circumvent the threshold limits defined in Section 2. Threshold compliance is determined by the total cumulative cost of the required work or asset procurement.

3.2 Procurement Sourcing Thresholds

  • Transactions under R10,000: A single written quote from an approved, accredited estate vendor.
  • Transactions from R10,001 to R50,000: A minimum of two independent written quotes must be evaluated.
  • Transactions over R50,000 / Capital Tenders: A minimum of three independent written quotes accompanied by a detailed comparison (system-generated) must be submitted to the Finance Committee.

4. Automated Workflow & Outbound Email Dispatch Engine

When a Purchase Order is submitted, 2_send_approvals_postgres.py evaluates the PO status and executes automated email dispatch based on the approval_permission designated in the approvers database table:

4.1 Multi-Stage Dispatch & Recipient Routing

  • Stage 1: Submit for Finance Review
    • Primary Recipients ("To"): All users where approval_permission = 'Finance Review'.
    • Copied Recipients ("CC"): All users where approval_permission = 'Estate Manager'.
    • Required Call-To-Action Text:
      Please review the purchase order details and reply directly to this message typing either "RECOMMEND FOR APPROVAL" or "RECOMMEND FOR REJECTION".
    • Post-Dispatch Status: Updates submission_status in po_log to Finance Review.
  • Stage 2: Submit for Approval
    • Primary Recipients ("To"): All users where approval_permission = 'Approval Authority'.
    • Copied Recipients ("CC"): All users where approval_permission = 'Finance Review' AND approval_permission = 'Estate Manager'.
    • Required Call-To-Action Text:
      Please review the purchase order details and reply directly to this message typing either "APPROVED" or "REJECTED".
    • Post-Dispatch Status: Updates submission_status in po_log to Sent.

4.2 AI Recommendation Persistence

To optimize execution speed and prevent re-running costly LLM analyses, the system checks whether an ai_recommendation_summary already exists in the database:

  • Initial Analysis: When sent for Submit for Finance Review, the Gemini AI Engine evaluates quotes, vendor OSINT records, and GL budget run-rates, storing the analysis in po_log.ai_recommendation_summary.
  • Summary Reuse: When the item transitions to Submit for Approval, the engine detects the existing summary and automatically reuses the exact AI audit output in the approval email body without re-triggering the Gemini API.

6. Executive Safeguards & Audit Control

  • Unalterable Audit Trails: Every outbound email dispatch and inbound reply is logged chronologically to the PostgreSQL workflow_control_log table and localized audit log file.
  • Context-Aware Parsing: The reply processor strips out historical email threads and splitters (e.g., -----Original Message-----) to evaluate only top-level replies from validated approvers.
  • Fiscal Runway Monitoring: Post-purchase balances are checked against the estate's 2-Month Run-rate Buffer Pool during AI synthesis to flag structural deficit risks before approval sign-offs occur.
