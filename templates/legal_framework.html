Statutory Motivation: Financial Controls & HSSE

Why implementing a Financial Authority Matrix and OHS Management System is legally mandatory for the Board
Board Briefing | Governance & Compliance

Companies Act

ACT 71 OF 2008
  • Sec 76 Fiduciary Duties: Directors must act in good faith, with care, skill, and reasonable diligence.
  • Personal Liability: Directors face personal financial liability for unapproved expenditure or losses.
  • Formal Delegation: Mandates explicit authorization limits and prohibits financial anti-splitting.

OHS Act & Safety

ACT 85 OF 1993
  • Sec 16(1) / 16(2) Liability: Statutory criminal and civil liability rests directly on Board & CEO/Management.
  • Sec 37(2) Contractors: HOA is legally liable for contractor workplace injuries without formal compliance.
  • HSSE Imperative: Systematic risk audits, incident logs, and mandatory estate OHS standards required.

CSOS Act & MOI

ACT 9 OF 2011 & GOVERNANCE
  • Financial Oversight: Statutory duty to ensure prudent management of estate operating & reserve funds.
  • Audit Trails: Mandatory procurement compliance (tiered quotes) and unalterable transaction logs.
  • Fiduciary Stewardship: Protecting owner levy value and mitigating dispute risks under CSOS oversight.

King IV Principles

CORPORATE GOVERNANCE CODE
  • Effective Controls: Mandates formal internal controls, risk governance, and segregation of duties.
  • Business Judgment Rule: Protects Directors against liability only if formal risk systems are active.
  • Transparency: Establishes ethical culture, audit integrity, and operational sustainability.

BOARD EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Operating without these systems exposes Directors to direct personal and statutory legal liability under the Companies Act and OHS Act. Implementing the Delegation of Financial Authority Matrix and HSSE Framework transitions Cathkin Estates from informal operational risk to full statutory compliance and governance protection.
